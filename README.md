(Features)
 Browse and search historical tourist spots
 Book a visit, with or without a local guide
 Leave reviews for spots and guides
 Spot owners can allow guided tours at their sites
 Managers verify users and spots
(User Roles)
 Tourist: Find spots, check availability, book guides, write reviews 
 Guide: Offer guiding services and set a rate per hour 
 Spot Owner: List a spot and decide whether guides are allowed 
 Manager: Verify users and spots, manage the app
