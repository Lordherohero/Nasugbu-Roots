 Features
- Browse, search, and view details of historical tourist spots
- Book a visit, with or without a local guide
- Cancel bookings, and see only your own bookings
- Leave reviews for spots and guides after a visit
- Guides confirm bookings and manage their rate and bio
- Spot owners add and edit their spots
- Managers verify spots and guides
- Register a new account as a tourist, guide, or spot owner

User Roles

| Role | What they can do |
|------|------------------|
| Tourist | Find spots, book visits, cancel bookings, review spots and guides |
| Guide | Confirm or cancel assigned bookings, update rate and bio, see reviews |
| Spot Owner | Add spots, edit details, fee, availability, and whether guides are allowed |
| Manager | Verify spots and guides |
